Events & Community

Community is at the core of what we do at Wheelie Good Bikes. Beyond repairs and sales, we organize group rides, hands-on workshops, and neighborhood gatherings — because cycling is more fun together. Whatever your experience level, there’s a place for you here.

Group Rides

Casual, no-drop rides through Rogers Park and the surrounding neighborhood. All paces and bike types welcome — cargo bikes, e-bikes, whatever you’ve got. We ride together and no one gets left behind.

Hands-On Workshops

From fixing a flat to understanding your drivetrain, our workshops give riders real, practical skills. Small groups, friendly instruction, and no such thing as a dumb question.

Workshops

Community Gatherings

Open houses, cargo bike meetups, and neighborhood events. A chance to hang out, meet other riders, and see what’s new at the shop — no agenda required.

Community

E-Bike & Cargo Clinics

Thinking about going electric or hauling more by bike? We host casual, pressure-free info sessions — what to look for, how to maintain them, and how to choose the right setup for your life.

Education

Women & Non-Binary Rides

A dedicated space for women and non-binary riders to build confidence, find community, and enjoy the ride. Welcoming to all experience levels.

Family & Kids Rides

Slow, easy, kid-friendly rides for families and anyone who prefers a relaxed pace. Trailers, tag-alongs, and cargo bikes all welcome.
