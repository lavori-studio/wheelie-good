Why Wheelie Good Bikes?

Our mission is simple yet powerful: to empower every rider through knowledge.

We create a space where cyclists of all experience levels are treated with respect, feel genuinely welcome, and leave with a deeper understanding of their bikes and their own capabilities. This philosophy shapes everything we do — from how we service bikes to how we educate our customers.

Wheelie Good Bikes isn’t just a retail store; it’s a center for learning, connection, and community.

We provide expert repairs, personalized consultations, and hands-on workshops that give riders confidence and independence. Whether someone is buying their first commuter bike, choosing an e-bike for daily use, or upgrading a family cargo setup, we guide them with integrity and care — helping them make informed, sustainable decisions.

By teaching as much as we repair, and listening as much as we sell, we build long-term trust and loyalty.

Our Values

Inclusivity

Every rider belongs here. No experience level, body type, budget, or background is unwelcome.

Education

We teach as much as we fix. Leaving with more knowledge than you arrived with is the goal.

Sustainability

Bikes are one of the most sustainable ways to get around. We’re proud to support that mission every day.

Community

Rogers Park is our home. Group rides, events, and genuine relationships are at the heart of what we do.

Expertise Without Elitism

We know a lot about bikes — and we never make you feel like you don’t.
