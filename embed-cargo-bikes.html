Essential Transportation for Real Life

For a lot of riders, a cargo bike isn’t a novelty — it’s the vehicle that actually gets you where you need to go. School drop-off, the grocery run, hauling gear for a weekend outing, or just carrying more than a standard bike ever could: a cargo bike can take over trips you’d otherwise make by car, and open up a simpler, more connected way to move through Rogers Park. Whether you’re carrying kids, groceries, tools, or a trunk’s worth of gear, we’re here to help you find the right setup and keep it running strong for years.

A New Way of Life, One Ride at a Time

Skip the Car Trips

School runs, grocery hauls, weekend errands — a cargo bike can handle the trips that used to mean digging out the car keys. Less time circling for parking, more time actually getting there.

Room for Whatever You’re Hauling

Longtail and box-style cargo bikes carry kids, groceries, gear, or all of it at once. No more choosing what fits — just load up and go.

A Simpler Daily Routine

No gas station, no parking search, no car payment creeping up. Plenty of riders find that once they make the switch, the cargo bike quietly becomes their default way to get around.

Fresh Air Over Traffic

Trade the inside of a car for the actual outdoors. Riding instead of driving means lower emissions and a commute that leaves you a little happier instead of a little more stressed.

Types of Cargo Bikes We Work With

Longtail

An extended rear rack turns a familiar bike shape into serious hauling capacity — great for kid seats, panniers, or a full week of groceries.

Box Bike (Bakfiets)

A front-mounted cargo box puts kids or cargo where you can see them. A favorite for riders who want company along for the ride, whether that’s a kid, a dog, or just a big grocery haul.

Mid-Tail

A middle ground between a standard bike and a full longtail — extra hauling room without sacrificing the nimble feel of everyday riding.
