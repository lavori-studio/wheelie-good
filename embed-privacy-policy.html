Privacy Policy

Wheelie Good Bikes respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or shop with us, how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, reach out any time — contact details are at the bottom of this page.

Information We Collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address when you place an order, book a service appointment, sign up for a workshop or group ride, or contact us with a question.

When you shop with us online, our e-commerce platform (Lightspeed) and its payment processor also collect order details like items purchased, order history, and shipping information. We may also collect basic technical information automatically, such as your browser type and pages visited, through standard website analytics.

Payment Information

We never see or store your full credit card number. Online payments are processed securely by Lightspeed's payment processor, and in-store payments are processed through our point-of-sale system. Both handle card data according to industry security standards (PCI DSS).

How We Use Your Information

  • To process orders, repairs, and service appointments
  • To contact you about your order, repair status, or appointment
  • To respond to questions or requests you send us
  • To send updates about workshops, group rides, or shop news — only if you've opted in
  • To improve our website and the services we offer

We do not sell your personal information to anyone, ever.

Email Communications

If you've signed up to hear from us, we may send occasional emails about shop news, events, or promotions. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can opt out at any time. We'll still need to email you about an active order or repair even if you've unsubscribed from marketing emails, since those are transactional, not promotional.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies — small files stored in your browser — to remember your cart, keep you logged in, and understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site (like checkout) may not work properly without them.

Third-Party Services

We work with a small number of trusted service providers to run our business, including:

  • Lightspeed — our e-commerce and point-of-sale platform, which hosts our online store and processes orders and payments
  • Shipping carriers, to deliver online orders
  • Email service providers, for shop newsletters and order notifications

These providers only receive the information they need to do their job, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information, including relying on Lightspeed's secure infrastructure for online orders and payment processing. That said, no method of storing or transmitting data online is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we'll remove it.

Data Retention

We keep order and service records for as long as needed for business, tax, and warranty purposes. You can ask us to delete other personal information we hold about you at any time, as described below.

Your Rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we have about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your personal information, where we're not required to keep it (such as for completed order records)
  • Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time

To make any of these requests, email us at info@wheeliegoodbikeschicago.com or call (773) 739-9119.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally as our business or applicable laws change. If we make significant changes, we'll post the updated policy here with a new effective date. We encourage you to check back periodically.
